Progression

Stay inside the rep range. When all sets hit the top of the range with clean form for two sessions, add a little weight. For assisted pull-ups, reduce assistance instead. Keep roughly 1–3 good reps in reserve on most sets.

Conditioning

Add 1–2 easy 30–45 minute runs, incline walks, cycles or swims per week. Use intervals sparingly and only if recovery stays good.

Body + nutrition

Judge progress from 7–14 day trends, not one weigh-in.

Nutrition anchor

Protein and vegetables/fruit first. Add carbohydrate according to training and appetite rather than banning it. Keep snacks, alcohol and liquid calories deliberate.
